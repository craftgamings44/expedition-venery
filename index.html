How it plays

  • It cannot see through rock. It hunts by sound alone — your pickaxe, your footsteps, your landing.
  • It learns. It remembers where it heard you and which way you fled, and it uses that next time.
  • Stay silent behind stone and it won't know you exist. In the open, it will see you.
  • You cannot dig upward. If it catches you, you lose your pack — and your body ends up in its lair.
