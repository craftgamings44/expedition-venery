Command shape:

```
mmx.cmd image generate --prompt "<prompt>" --width <W> --height <H> --seed <SEED> --out <file>
```

- **Backdrops** — 1024x512, deliberately BRIGHT and evenly lit (they sit behind the tiles under the
  game's dark light-radius mask, so dark art would be invisible in-game).

## Post-processing note

`banner_shale.png` (seed 5214) was generated with a solid black letterbox band of 65 rows at the top
and 52 rows at the bottom — the generator added cinematic bars despite explicit anti-letterbox
prompt wording (seeds 5201 and 5211 were also rejected for this reason). Those bars were removed
deterministically with a row-profile crop (keep rows 66..667) and the result was rescaled back to
1280x720 with HighQualityBicubic. No other file was post-processed.
